Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as:

  • The user's account identifier.
  • The user's email.
  • The user's name.
  • The user's custom lists, saved movies and TV shows, favorites, and shared list membership.

  • The user's favorited streaming services.

Third Party Access

The Application uses backend services operated by the Service Provider to provide account, search, watchlist, recommendation, and streaming availability features. The Service Provider may share or transmit information with third parties in the ways that are described in this privacy statement.


The Application uses third-party content data providers to retrieve movie and TV metadata, images, streaming availability, prices, provider names, provider logos, and provider links. To fulfill searches and content detail requests, the Application may send search terms, movie or TV show identifiers, country, and language parameters to those providers through the Service Provider's backend. The Service Provider does not send the user's email, name, account identifier, custom lists, favorites, or profile preferences to those content data providers for those lookups.


If the user opens a third-party streaming provider link, the user leaves the Application and the destination service handles information according to its own privacy policy and terms.
